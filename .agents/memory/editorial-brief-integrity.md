---
name: Editorial brief integrity
description: Why follow-up complaints must retain user constraints and long cuts must review their full chosen windows.
---

Treat prior user instructions as the editorial brief; assistant acknowledgments
and similarity matches are not evidence of compliance.

**Why:** Complaints such as “why are you not giving me a cut with those
parameters?” were searched literally, while a short history window dropped
the original air-year and exclusion requirements. The assistant then
misrepresented spoken-year references and visual matches as verified dates.

**How to apply:** Resolve follow-ups against user instructions, keep explicit
source exclusions as well as named-person exclusions, and use original-air-date
metadata rather than dialogue or appearance. Do not claim to exclude an
unidentified person with certainty.

An omitted model field is not permission to clear an explicit date or
exclusion requirement. Keep explicit constraints independently enforceable.

**Why:** A model returned a null air-year field while retaining a year-specific
objective, silently widening a restricted search to almost the entire library.
Final verification caught the off-year sources; the revision was marked
invalid and the previously verified cut restored without deleting history.

**How to apply:** Preserve explicit user constraints when structured extraction
omits them, validate final source eligibility, and never reinterpret missing
model output as a user-requested relaxation.

Choose long story windows only after exposing their full included transcript
to editorial selection. Do not expand short selected hits into unreviewed
surrounding footage just to reach a runtime.

**Why:** A short assembly was padded to 55 minutes while its generated reply
still described a 15-minute selection. The displayed target disguised the
lack of reviewed content.

**How to apply:** Stop a candidate window before the transcript review budget
is exhausted; persist the measured cut duration and report shortfalls honestly.
Preserve prior revisions when rebuilding so editorial choices remain recoverable.
